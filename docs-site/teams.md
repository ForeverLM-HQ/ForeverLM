# Teams

A team is a second library that several people share: the same papers, the same
notebook, the same Pages, a transcript of a chat worth passing on, and a task board
that says who does what. Your own library stays yours. A team sits beside it, and
you switch between the two.

## Sharing a notebook

The quickest way to share is from the notebook itself. Click **Share** at the top
right of an open notebook, or choose **Share…** from the notebook's **⋯** menu,
its menu in the Space, or its row in the sidebar.

- **A notebook you have not shared yet.** The popover says what goes: the
  notebook and its sources. Choose **Invite People…** to invite people by name or
  email through Apple's sharing sheet, or **Copy Invitation Link** to copy a link
  you can send anyone. Either one first makes a team named after the notebook and
  moves the notebook into it, its sources with it (they stay in your library
  too). From then on it is the team's notebook, and what anyone adds to it is
  there for everyone ([Sharing into a team](#sharing-into-a-team)).
- **A notebook already in a team.** The popover lists who has it, the team's
  members and the people invited, with **Invite People…** and **Copy Invitation
  Link** for that team, and **Share with Team** for another team.
- **A team you can only view.** You see who has the notebook; inviting is the
  owner's. A notebook of your own can still go to another team with **Share
  with Team**.

The copied link opens the team to anyone who has it, with editing. To choose who
may only view, use **Invite People…** or the team's **Manage Members**.

In the [Web Library](/web-library), **Share** at a notebook's top right shows
who has the notebook and gives **Copy Invitation Link**; a notebook you have not
shared yet is shared by your Mac, so ForeverLM must be open there. On the
iPhone, **Share** beside a notebook's **⋯** offers **Share with Others…**: your
Mac moves the notebook into a shared space the next time ForeverLM runs there,
and after that **Manage Sharing…** opens Apple's sharing sheet, to invite people
or copy the link.

### Before you share

The first time you share anything that gives other people copies of your
sources, ForeverLM asks you to confirm that you have the right to share them, in
line with copyright and each source's terms. Tick **I have the rights to share
these sources** and choose **Share**. You are asked once for your account, on
your Macs, your iPhone and the web; you would be asked again only if the wording
changes.

## What a team is

A team lives in the iCloud account of the person who created it, as one shared
area in that account's ForeverLM storage. Members receive it through Apple's
iCloud sharing, the same mechanism Notes and Pages use to share a document.
ForeverLM keeps no directory of teams and no server copy of anyone's content.

Three consequences follow:

- **Storage counts against the creator's iCloud.** Members' quotas are untouched
  no matter how many papers they share into the team.
- **Regular Apple Accounts are all that is needed.** Managed Apple Accounts are
  not required. An organization that turns off iCloud sharing on its managed
  accounts keeps those members out; that is the organization's policy, not
  ForeverLM's.
- **The roster is Apple's participant list.** Who is in the team and what each
  person may do is read from the share, not from anything ForeverLM stores.

## Creating a team and inviting people

A notebook's **Share** makes a team for you ([Sharing a
notebook](#sharing-a-notebook)). To make one yourself, click your name at the
bottom of the navigation rail, open **Library**,
choose **New Team…** and give it a name, or open
**My Settings → Data & Privacy → Teams**, type the team's name and choose
**Create**. The team appears in the library switcher at once, empty, and the
app switches into it.

While a team is open, the account menu carries a second settings entry named
after it — **Mitochondria Settings**, say — beside **My Settings**. It opens the
team's own pages: **General** (name, invitation link, leave or delete) and
**Members** (the roster). The same two pages sit in the Settings sidebar under
the team's name for as long as that team is the open library; switch to another
team and they name that one, switch to My Library and they go away. **Data & Privacy →
Teams** still lists every team you belong to, whichever library is open.

**Manage Members**, on either team page, opens Apple's sharing sheet. Invite
people by email address or phone number, and choose whether they may **edit** or
only **view**. Apple sends the invitation; the person accepts it on a Mac or an
iPhone running ForeverLM and the team appears in their switcher. You can change
a member's access or remove them from the same sheet at any time, and rename the
team on its General page.

**Copy Link**, beside Manage Members and on the General page, copies the team's
invitation link. When the owner copies it, the team is opened to anyone who has
the link, with editing, so the link works for a person you did not name; use
Manage Members instead to invite named people or to let someone only view. A
member's Copy Link copies the link as it stands.

## Switching libraries

Click your name at the bottom of the navigation rail. The account menu opens
with one **Library** row naming the library on screen; click it and the menu
turns into the switcher: **My Library**, then each team by name (with a lock on
one you can only view), then **New Team…**. While a team is open, a badge beside
your name carries the team's name (with a lock when you can only view it), so
the rail always says whose library is on screen. Switching reloads notebooks,
Pages and Chats for that library; everything else in the Studio works as before,
and a team badge marks a row whose home is a team wherever one is shown. The
iPhone, the iPad and the [Web Library](/web-library) list team notebooks beside
your own.

Your sources stay with you. While a team is open, the Library still lists every
source of your own, plus any source the team holds that you don't (one a
teammate shared, or one that came with a teammate's Page or course). A team's
copy of a source you already have shows once, as your own. A team is for
sharing work: notebooks, courses, Pages, linked pages and chat transcripts.

A notebook you make or a Page you write while a team is open belongs to the team
from the start. A URL you add is yours, like any other source. When you add one
of your sources to a team notebook, the team gets its copy of that source.

## Sharing into a team

A notebook shared into a team you own moves there. Everything else, and anything
shared into a team someone else owns, is copied.

- **A notebook moves into your own team.** It is the team's from then on, and you
  and every member work in that one notebook: what anyone adds to it is simply
  there for everyone, and nothing drifts apart. The sources it holds stay in
  your library too, and the team keeps its own copy of each.
  Your reviews, quizzes and memory reviews in it stay in your own library. Your
  Mac does the move, so a share from the iPhone or the web waits for ForeverLM to
  run there.
- **Anything else is a copy.** The team gets its own copy, and your original
  stays exactly where it was, and stays yours.

Every source row, notebook row and open notebook's **⋯** menu, Page menu and
chat row in My Library, and the **⋯** menu of each of your courses, has a
**Share with Team ▸**
item (your own source rows and courses keep it while a team is open) listing
the teams you may write into. The first time, ForeverLM asks you to confirm
that you may share the sources it copies ([Before you
share](#before-you-share)); sharing a chat's transcript copies no source and
asks nothing.

- **Sources.** A source keeps the same identity in every library, so a paper
  two members share is one team record, not two. Sharing the same source again
  updates the team's copy rather than adding a second one. A paper's PDF or a
  book's file travels with the copy, so deleting the original from your library
  never takes the team's copy away. A book, meeting series or podcast is shared one item
  at a time; the collection itself does not travel.
- **Notebooks.** Into a team you own, the notebook moves (above). A copy into
  someone else's team carries the notebook's instructions, research questions
  and Goals, its sources, its Pages and the chats you had in it, each chat as a
  read-only transcript filed under the team's copy of the notebook.
- **Pages.** A Page shared with a team needs no publishing and no reader list. The
  team's copy syncs into every member's ForeverLM and opens there as a page. To
  change a team's Page, **Edit** makes your own draft (**Continue Draft** goes
  back to it) and **Publish** puts your changes into the team's Page; until then
  the team reads it as it was, and **Discard Draft** drops yours. Publishing to
  foreverlm.com remains the route for people outside the team, and the two
  actions sit apart in the menu.
- **Sites, animations and linked pages.** A Claude artifact or a Codex site
  shared with a team is the page itself: the team's copy keeps the address
  and lists under the team's Sites, and sharing a page linked again updates
  that one entry rather than adding another. A ForeverLM site or an animation
  shared with a team travels with its files into the team's library, lists
  under the team's Sites or Animations, and follows every change you save.
- **Chats.** A chat shared with a team is a read-only transcript. See
  [In-App Chat](/in-app-chat#sharing-a-chat-with-a-team) for what travels and
  what is stripped.
- **Courses.** A course shared with a team travels whole: its lectures, their
  readings and material, its quizzes, and every source on its path, copied into
  the team with their files. Each member finds it under the Space's
  **Courses**, marked with the team, and takes it at their own pace: their own lectures
  watched, their own quiz scores and passes, and the same gate that holds back
  what follows a quiz until they pass it. Opening it switches to the team's
  library. Your own progress through the course stays in My Library, and the
  team's copy starts at the beginning for everyone. Sharing the course again
  brings the team's copy up to date; a quiz a teammate wrote on it stays.

## What follows a shared notebook

A notebook you shared into your own team is the team's notebook, so nothing
has to follow it: what anyone files in it is there for every member, on every
device. A source of your own that you add to it is copied into the team, by
your Mac.

A copy you gave a team someone else owns takes nothing more. What you file
under your original afterwards stays in your original, and nothing a member
does to the team's copy changes yours.

## Team notebooks and the task board

A notebook's task board is the **Tasks** section of its **Notebook settings** in
the [Web Library](/web-library) (click the notebook's name or its **⋯**), and an
assistant works it over MCP (below); the Mac app shows no board. In a team
notebook the board is the team's: every member sees the same tasks.

A task has a title, notes, a status (**To do**, **In progress**, **Done**), an
optional due date, and can point at the source or Page it is about. In a team
notebook a task can be assigned to a member from the roster. The assignment
survives the roster changing: a task still shows who it belonged to after that
person has left the team.

Tasks also exist on your own notebooks, without assignment.

An AI assistant connected over MCP can work the same board with
`list_notebook_tasks`, `create_notebook_task`, `update_notebook_task` and
`delete_notebook_task`, can create teams and share into them with
`list_teams`, `create_team` and `share_with_team`, and can share a notebook in
one step with `share_notebook`, which returns the invitation link. Until you
have confirmed that you may share your sources, an assistant must ask you first
and pass your answer. These tools run on the Mac only; see the [MCP Tools
Reference](/mcp-tools).

## What stays private

A team shares material, not your progress through it. The following never enter
a team record:

- where you got to in a paper, whether you have read it, and how deeply you
  review it;
- what you completed in a team notebook and your quiz history there;
- which lectures of a team's course you watched, your quiz scores and passes in
  it, and whether you hid it;
- your highlights, memory items and review results;
- your Schedule.

Two members reading the same team paper each keep their own place in it, and
deleting a paper from the team does not delete your notes about it. Your
reviews, quizzes and memory reviews stay in your own library even when they are
filed in a team notebook, and scores and grades never enter a team.

## Read-only members

A member the owner gave **view** access sees the team's library and its task
boards but cannot change them. The controls are disabled, and any change that
would land in a read-only team is refused rather than queued.

## Leaving and deleting a team

**Leave Team** (a member) removes the team from your Macs. **Delete Team** (the
owner) deletes the team for everyone, with everything in it, a notebook you
moved into it included. What a member shared from their own library was a copy,
so their original is still in their library, exactly as it was before they
shared it.

If the owner removes you, the team disappears from your switcher and the Studio
returns to My Library.

## Limits in this release

- **Teams are made on the Mac.** **New Team…** and a team's settings pages are
  the Mac's. The [Web Library](/web-library) can switch to a team, read its
  content and work its task boards, but cannot manage a team's members; on the
  iPhone, a shared notebook's **Manage Sharing…** opens Apple's sharing sheet.
  A notebook's **Share** on the web or the iPhone asks your Mac to share the
  notebook, so it happens once ForeverLM runs on your Mac.
- **The Mac does not show everything in a team yet.** The team's notebooks and
  their sources reach every member's Mac, but Pages, chats, courses (a shared
  course included) and tasks kept in the team are not shown on another
  member's Mac yet.
- **Chats are shared as transcripts.** There is no live multi-person chat;
  collaboration on prose is a Page.
- **Nothing moves back out.** There is no unshare yet: a notebook you moved into
  your own team stays the team's. A copy can be deleted in the team's library
  without touching your original.
- **Audio narration is not shared.** Each member's Mac generates its own Text to
  Speech audio for a team copy.
