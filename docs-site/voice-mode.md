# Voice Mode

Voice mode is a spoken conversation with ForeverLM, the way ChatGPT's voice mode works: you talk, it answers out loud within a moment, and you can cut in at any time. Use it to talk a paper through, to be quizzed on it, or to talk to ForeverLM as your assistant about your work, your goals and your plan, in the app or in the car.

Voice mode runs on OpenAI's realtime voice model, paid for by ForeverLM. It needs Sign in with Apple and nothing else: no key, and nothing is billed to you or to your OpenRouter key. Each account has a generous daily allowance of conversations.

It is all one model: ForeverLM hears you and answers in its own voice. No separate speech-to-text or text-to-speech runs, and nothing is shown as text while you talk.

## Talk about a source

- **iPhone:** in the Reader, tap the **waveform** in the bottom bar to talk about the source. Hold it and choose **Quiz Me** to start with a question.
- **Mac:** in the Reader's **⋯**, choose **Talk About It** or **Quiz Me Out Loud**. The conversation opens in a small floating window that stays above the Reader; close it to end.

ForeverLM has the whole source in front of it (a very long book is searched as you go), so it can explain a result, check what you remember, and point to the passage. When it quizzes you it asks one question at a time, tells you plainly what was right and what was missing, and goes deeper as you get it right.

## Talk to ForeverLM

The assistant is the same conversation about you instead of one source. When it starts it reads, on your device:

- your plan Page for this quarter,
- your calendar for the week (where your week and day plans live),
- what is on your Schedule,
- your goals and notebooks,
- what you read, listened to, watched or discussed in the last two weeks, and your latest chats.

Ask it what to work on next, to go over your day, or about a paper you read last week. It can find any source in your library, open it, and talk about it or quiz you on it, and it looks things up again when the picture it started with is not enough. It never changes your library, plan or calendar.

On the iPhone and in CarPlay, ask for "my review" or "my cards" and it goes through today's cards out loud: it asks each card, tells you whether you had it, and records Remembered or Forgot just as the card review does.

- **iPhone:** the **waveform** at the top of Home.
- **Mac:** **Chat › Talk to ForeverLM**.
- **CarPlay:** open ForeverLM and start talking. The assistant greets you with one useful thing, such as what is next in your plan or the paper you were last into, and keeps every answer short enough to follow while driving.

On the iPhone, the first time the assistant starts it asks for access to your calendar. Without it, the assistant still knows everything else.

## While you talk

- **Interrupt** whenever you like; it stops and listens, and it knows how much of its answer you heard.
- **Mute** the microphone from the voice screen.
- **AirPods, CarPlay and speakers** can change mid-conversation; the audio follows.
- **A dropped connection** reconnects on its own and picks up where you were. If it cannot come back, **Resume** starts again.
- **End**, or say goodbye: ForeverLM says goodbye and ends the conversation.

Every conversation is saved as a voice chat: a source conversation with that source's chats, filed in the notebook you opened it from, and an assistant conversation under your chats. The chat keeps what ForeverLM said; your own turns show as spoken, because your words are never transcribed. It syncs to your other devices like any chat.

## Choose the voice

Pick the voice ForeverLM speaks in from ten: Marin (the default), Cedar, Alloy, Ash, Ballad, Coral, Echo, Sage, Shimmer and Verse. On the Mac it is **Settings** -> **Voice** -> **Voice mode**; on the iPhone, **Settings** -> **Voice Mode**. One pick serves the Mac, the iPhone and CarPlay, and your next conversation speaks in it; a conversation under way keeps its voice. A chat can set it too (`voice_mode.voice`). Every voice costs the same: nothing, to you.

## Usage

Voice conversations are listed in AI Usage on the Mac and in **Settings › Usage** on the iPhone, task **Voice Mode**, with a **ForeverLM credits** capsule: the cost shown is what OpenAI charged ForeverLM, and nothing is billed to you.
