# ForeverLM vs Elicit

Elicit is an AI research assistant for finding literature, screening papers, extracting structured evidence, and producing research reports. ForeverLM starts later in the lifecycle: it keeps the papers and other sources you have chosen, lets you work across them, and helps you understand and remember them.

## At a glance

| | ForeverLM | Elicit |
|---|---|---|
| **Price** | 7 days free, then $19 once (no subscription); AI on your own OpenRouter key at cost | Free Basic; Plus $132/year; Pro $480/year; Scale $1,068/year |
| **Core job** | Persistent source library and learning workflow | Literature discovery, screening, extraction, and systematic review |
| **Corpus** | Your papers, articles, books, podcasts, videos, and other sources | Search across more than 138 million papers plus imports and uploads |
| **AI output** | Source-grounded conversation and review | Reports, evidence tables, extraction columns, screening, and paper chat |
| **Organization** | Notebooks, Schedule, status, source relationships, and review history | Research workflows, tables, reports, alerts, and review notebooks |
| **Citations** | Keeps paper metadata and source links | Evidence-backed answers and research exports, not cite-while-you-write |
| **Storage** | Local files and SQLite in your iCloud | Cloud service |

*Elicit pricing and limits checked against its [official pricing page](https://elicit.com/pricing) in August 2026.*

## Where Elicit is better

- **Finding the evidence.** Search a vast scholarly corpus without first assembling a library.
- **Systematic reviews.** Dedicated screening and extraction workflows can process thousands of papers with explicit criteria.
- **Structured synthesis.** Custom columns make it easy to compare methods, populations, interventions, and outcomes.
- **Research reports.** Elicit can build an evidence-backed answer from many academic sources and expose its supporting papers.
- **Team and API options.** Higher plans support larger workflows, collaboration, alerts, and programmatic access.

## Where ForeverLM is better

- **Your chosen sources persist together.** Papers live beside the articles, talks, podcasts, books, and meetings that shaped the same notebook.
- **The library belongs to you.** Files and metadata remain local and in your private iCloud rather than requiring a hosted research workspace.
- **AI is model-flexible.** Use several providers or an existing assistant subscription through MCP.
- **Reading and listening are first-class.** Work through the complete source, not only the extracted evidence table.
- **Learning continues after synthesis.** Schedule sources and test your own explanation through a saved review.

## Which should you choose?

Choose **Elicit** when the bottleneck is finding, screening, and extracting evidence for a literature or systematic review.

Choose **ForeverLM** when the bottleneck is managing everything you have selected and turning it into durable understanding.

The tools are complementary: discover and screen in Elicit, export the papers that matter, then learn them in ForeverLM.
