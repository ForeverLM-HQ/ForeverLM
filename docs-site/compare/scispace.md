# ForeverLM vs SciSpace

SciSpace is an end-to-end academic research platform for discovering literature, running deep reviews, chatting with PDFs, generating citations, and assisting with academic writing. ForeverLM is a personal research library for the sources you actually keep, including material beyond scholarly papers, with listening, planning, and active review built in.

## At a glance

| | ForeverLM | SciSpace |
|---|---|---|
| **Price** | 30 days free, then $19 once (no subscription); AI on your own OpenRouter key at cost | Free Basic with credits; Premium starts at $12/month billed annually |
| **Core job** | Long-term source library and learning loop | Academic search, literature review, PDF explanation, and writing tools |
| **Discovery** | Imports and plugins; not a scholarly search engine | Search and agentic review across scientific literature |
| **AI** | Configurable models, cross-source Chat, and MCP assistants | Literature Review, Deep Review, Chat with PDF, paraphrasing, and AI detection |
| **Sources** | Papers, articles, books, podcasts, videos, courses, and more | Primarily scholarly literature and academic writing |
| **Storage** | Local SQLite and files in private iCloud | Cloud workspace |
| **Review** | Scheduled review dialogue with saved history | Research synthesis rather than learner assessment |

*SciSpace product scope and credit pricing checked against its [official pricing](https://scispace.com/pricing) and [credit guide](https://scispace.com/resources/credits-pricing-guide/) in August 2026.*

## Where SciSpace is better

- **Literature discovery.** It searches beyond your own files and can assemble a review from published research.
- **Academic workflow breadth.** Search, explain, paraphrase, cite, detect AI-written text, and format manuscripts in one web platform.
- **PDF explanation.** Its Copilot-style experience is focused specifically on making academic language and figures easier to understand.
- **Deep research automation.** Agent credits fund longer searches and reviews that would be manual library work in ForeverLM.
- **Web access.** It works wherever a browser is available rather than requiring a Mac for the full experience.

## Where ForeverLM is better

- **A broader definition of research.** The decisive source may be a podcast interview, lecture, book, article, or meeting, not another journal paper.
- **Persistent ownership.** The library is stored locally and through your Apple account.
- **Conversation can change the library.** MCP assistants can organize notebooks, schedule work, and save reviews instead of only returning an answer.
- **Listening is part of reading.** Papers and articles can be narrated without leaving the source workflow.
- **Understanding is the outcome.** ForeverLM asks you to explain the material back and records where your explanation broke down.

## Which should you choose?

Choose **SciSpace** for literature discovery, automated academic review, PDF explanation, and writing assistance.

Choose **ForeverLM** for a private, durable research library that joins papers with the rest of your intellectual life and helps you retain what you read.
