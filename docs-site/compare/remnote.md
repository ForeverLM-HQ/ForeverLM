# ForeverLM vs RemNote

RemNote is an all-in-one student platform: notes that auto-generate flashcards, PDF annotation, exam schedulers, and a growing AI layer. It's arguably the most feature-complete study app for coursework. ForeverLM is narrower and more opinionated: track every source you consume, schedule it, and verify understanding through an AI review dialogue — with your data in your own iCloud and the AI you already pay for.

## At a glance

| | ForeverLM | RemNote |
|---|---|---|
| **Price** | Free, MCP included; AI on your own OpenRouter key at cost | Free tier (capped); Pro $96/yr; Pro + AI $216/yr; $395 lifetime (AI still +$10/mo) |
| **AI costs** | Your own OpenRouter key, billed by OpenRouter at cost with nothing added, for any model in Chat (free models cost nothing); or an AI subscription you already pay for, over MCP | Metered AI credits included in tiers (1,000/mo on Pro, 20,000/mo on Pro + AI); no bring-your-own-key |
| **Platforms** | Native macOS app (macOS 14+) | Web, Mac, Windows, Linux, iOS, Android |
| **Data storage** | Local SQLite + files in your private iCloud container | RemNote's cloud by default; local-only knowledge bases on desktop lose sync |
| **Source ingestion** | Automatic: articles, papers, podcasts, books, YouTube, X posts | PDFs, slides, lecture recordings, web clipper — no podcasts or YouTube |
| **Review method** | Review dialogue with an AI tutor | Spaced-repetition flashcards (SM-2/FSRS), AI-generated cards, AI quizzes and grading |
| **Model choice** | Any MCP-compatible AI (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok), or any model in-app through your own OpenRouter key | OpenAI/Anthropic/Google behind RemNote's credit system |
| **Export** | Files on your Mac; SQLite you can open | Markdown/JSON export; rich structure and scheduling history don't survive cleanly |

*Pricing verified July 2026 — check [remnote.com/pricing](https://www.remnote.com/pricing) for current numbers.*

## Pricing: subscription + credits vs a free app + your own AI key

Meaningful AI use on RemNote costs **$216/yr** (Pro + AI), and even the $395 lifetime license excludes AI credits — AI stays a $10/mo add-on forever. The credits are RemNote-denominated: you can't bring your own key or your existing ChatGPT/Claude subscription.

ForeverLM is **free**, and its AI takes two routes, mixable: any model in the in-app Chat, on your own OpenRouter key, billed by OpenRouter at its cost with nothing added (free models cost nothing); or drive ForeverLM over MCP from the Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or Grok subscription you're already paying for. The inference is on that subscription, and MCP itself is free. Per-conversation costs in the app are shown to the cent.

Three years of RemNote Pro + AI is ~$648 committed up front. ForeverLM's equivalent number is $0, MCP included, plus whatever OpenRouter bills for the AI you chose to run. That is the honest answer, not a dodge: usage is the one line item you control directly.

## Where RemNote is better

- **Course-based studying.** Annotating lecture slides and PDFs, auto-deriving flashcards from your note outline, exam schedulers that compress reviews before a test date — for a semester with exams, RemNote is purpose-built.
- **Every platform.** Web, Windows, Linux, Android, iOS. ForeverLM is Mac-only.
- **Integrated notes.** RemNote is a real outliner/note-taking app; ForeverLM deliberately isn't.
- **Usable free tier.** Unlimited notes, flashcards, and devices for $0 (with caps on PDFs and AI).
- **Built-in flashcard scheduling.** If you want classic spaced repetition with image occlusion and clozes, it's all there.

## Where ForeverLM is better

- **Explaining beats grading cards.** RemNote's review is still fundamentally flashcards — even its AI grading scores your answer to a fixed card. ForeverLM's review is a conversation: you explain the source from scratch and the AI probes gaps it discovers along the way. See [Why Quizzing?](/why-quizzing)
- **Your whole consumption stream, not just coursework.** Podcasts with full transcripts, Safari reading list articles, books, papers, YouTube, X posts — auto-discovered, not manually uploaded. RemNote has no podcast or YouTube ingestion at all.
- **Real money, not a private currency.** RemNote credits are RemNote-denominated, refilled monthly, and spendable nowhere else. ForeverLM's in-app Chat runs on your own OpenRouter key, billed at what OpenRouter actually charges with nothing added, or on free models for nothing; or lean on a subscription you already have, over MCP. Any model, switch anytime, and the ledger is in dollars you can check.
- **Data in your iCloud.** RemNote's synced knowledge bases live on their servers (local-only mode exists but gives up sync). ForeverLM syncs through your own Apple account; the app's servers never store your library.
- **Simpler by design.** RemNote is famously dense — rems, portals, descriptors, templates. ForeverLM has one loop: track, schedule, read or listen, explain it back.

## Which should you choose?

Choose **RemNote** if you're a student working through structured courses with exams, want notes + flashcards + PDF annotation in one place, or need Windows/Android.

Choose **ForeverLM** if you're on a Mac and your learning is source-driven — articles, papers, podcasts, books — and you'd rather own your data outright and pay for AI by the token you actually spent, instead of renting credits by the year.
