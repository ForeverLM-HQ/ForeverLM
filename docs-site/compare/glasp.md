# ForeverLM vs Glasp

Glasp is a social web highlighter for collecting passages and notes from articles, PDFs, YouTube, and Kindle, then exporting or sharing what you found. Its AI features include summaries, transcription, private highlights on paid plans, and an AI clone built from your collection. ForeverLM stores complete sources rather than centering the highlight, and uses them for cross-source research and active review.

## At a glance

| | ForeverLM | Glasp |
|---|---|---|
| **Price** | 30 days free, then $19 once (no subscription); AI on your own OpenRouter key at cost | Free tier; Pro $15/month or $150/year; Unlimited $36/month or $360/year |
| **Primary object** | The complete source and its learning history | The highlight, note, summary, and public knowledge profile |
| **Capture** | Articles, papers, books, podcasts, videos, courses, meetings, and more | Web articles, PDFs, YouTube, and Kindle highlights |
| **Social layer** | Private personal library | Public profiles, following, discovery, and shareable highlights |
| **AI** | Configurable cross-source Chat and MCP assistants | Summaries, transcription, recommendations, and an AI clone |
| **Export** | Source files plus structured database and plugin access | Exports to Notion, Obsidian, Markdown, and other note systems |
| **Review** | Scheduled review dialogue with saved history | Highlight resurfacing and AI interaction without the same review record |

*Glasp pricing checked against its [May 2026 pricing announcement](https://blog.glasp.co/price-update-2026-05-01/) and product materials in August 2026.*

## Where Glasp is better

- **Highlight capture.** The browser extension makes it fast to save and annotate passages without leaving the page.
- **Social discovery.** Public profiles and shared highlights expose what other curious readers found valuable.
- **Knowledge export.** Strong Notion, Obsidian, and Markdown workflows suit people who already maintain a note system.
- **YouTube and Kindle workflows.** Highlights and summaries are built around popular reading and viewing surfaces.
- **Shareable artifacts.** Quoteshots and public collections make excerpts easy to publish.

## Where ForeverLM is better

- **Complete sources remain available.** Chat and review can inspect the full paper, article, transcript, or book rather than only highlighted passages.
- **Privacy is the default.** The library lives in your iCloud instead of starting from a public knowledge profile.
- **More source types share one workflow.** Podcasts, courses, meetings, and academic papers are first-class records.
- **AI can organize and schedule.** MCP assistants do more than summarize or imitate a knowledge profile.
- **The learner must explain.** Review asks for reconstruction in your own words and records the result.

## Which should you choose?

Choose **Glasp** if highlighting, public knowledge sharing, and exporting excerpts into a note system are the center of your workflow.

Choose **ForeverLM** if you want a private library of complete sources with cross-source chat, listening, planning, and active review.
