# ForeverLM vs EndNote

EndNote is Clarivate's established reference manager for large scholarly libraries, full-text retrieval, citation formatting, collaboration, and manuscript workflows. EndNote 2025 adds an AI Research Assistant. ForeverLM is not a citation-production replacement. It focuses on collecting the sources you are actively learning from and connecting them to chat, listening, planning, and review.

## At a glance

| | ForeverLM | EndNote 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| **Price** | Free; MCP is 7 days free, then $19 once (no subscription); AI on your own OpenRouter key at cost | $275 full license; $150 student; $125 upgrade |
| **Primary job** | Source library and active learning | Professional reference management and academic writing |
| **Citations** | Metadata storage without cite-while-you-write | Cite While You Write and more than 6,000 bibliographic styles |
| **AI** | Configurable source chat and external MCP assistants | EndNote Research Assistant for paper and library questions |
| **Library** | Many learning formats in notebooks | Large scholarly reference collections and PDFs |
| **Storage** | Local SQLite and private iCloud files | Desktop library with EndNote Web sync and sharing |
| **Review** | Scheduled review dialogue with saved history | No equivalent source-understanding record |

*EndNote pricing checked against its [official purchase page](https://endnote.com/buy) in August 2026.*

## Where EndNote is better

- **Citation production.** EndNote remains a specialist for inserting, formatting, and changing references across manuscripts.
- **Style breadth.** Thousands of maintained bibliographic styles support journals and institutions around the world.
- **Large academic libraries.** Deduplication, metadata updates, full-text retrieval, groups, and search serve long-running research programs.
- **Publisher workflow.** Journal matching and Clarivate integrations help researchers move toward submission.
- **Institutional adoption.** Universities and labs can license, teach, and support a familiar standard tool.

## Where ForeverLM is better

- **It follows the learning process.** Capture, read, listen, ask, schedule, and review without leaving the source record.
- **Research includes more than citations.** Podcasts, courses, videos, books, articles, and meetings share context with academic papers.
- **AI is not tied to one vendor.** Choose the model and use external assistants through MCP.
- **The tutor asks you questions.** A review session evaluates your explanation instead of only answering a query.
- **A lower one-time price.** ForeverLM itself is free, so you can build the library without buying a $275 desktop license; only MCP costs anything ($19 once after 7 free days).

## Which should you choose?

Choose **EndNote** if citation accuracy, journal styles, manuscript integration, and institutional scholarly workflows are non-negotiable.

Choose **ForeverLM** if you need to understand and retain sources across formats rather than produce a final bibliography.

For many academics the right answer is both: EndNote owns the canonical references, while ForeverLM owns the active reading and learning.
