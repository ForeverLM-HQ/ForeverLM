# ForeverLM vs Speechify

Speechify is a specialist text-to-speech app: give it a webpage, PDF, document, or scan and it turns the text into polished audio on almost any device. ForeverLM can also read articles, papers, books, and other text sources aloud, but listening is one step in a larger workflow: save the source, schedule it, read or listen, ask questions, and then test whether you understood it.

The short version: choose Speechify when the voice reader itself is the product. Choose ForeverLM when listening should stay connected to the rest of your learning.

## At a glance

| | ForeverLM | Speechify |
|---|---|---|
| **Price** | Free, MCP included; AI and voices on your own OpenRouter key at cost (free voices cost nothing) | Free voice-reader tier; Premium is $29/month on the monthly plan |
| **Platforms** | Mac for the full library and reader; synced listening and voice interaction on iPhone | Web, Mac, Windows, iPhone, iPad, Android, Chrome, and Edge |
| **What it reads** | Articles, papers, books, and transcript-only sources already organized in your ForeverLM library | Webpages, PDFs, documents, scans, and text imported from cloud drives |
| **Voices** | Every speech model OpenRouter serves, from many makers; free ones cost nothing | Premium advertises 1,000+ voices in 60+ languages |
| **Playback** | Pause, 15-second seek, 0.75–2x speed, cached audio, iCloud sync | Up to 1.5x on Free and 5x on Premium, with synchronized highlighting |
| **AI conversation** | Source-grounded Chat inside the app, or Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Grok through MCP | Premium AI summaries, chat, and a general voice assistant |
| **After listening** | Put the source on Schedule, run a review, and keep the review linked to it | No learning schedule or source-linked active-recall history |
| **Storage** | Source files and the library live locally and in your private iCloud container | Account-based cloud service with cloud-drive integrations |

*Speechify features and pricing checked against its [official pricing page](https://speechify.com/pricing/) in August 2026. Plans change, so check the current offer before subscribing.*

## Speechify is a voice reader. ForeverLM is a learning loop.

Both apps can turn a paper or article into audio. What happens around the audio is different.

In Speechify, the main job is frictionless listening: import nearly any text, choose from a very large voice catalog, and play it across devices at high speed. Its scanning, browser extensions, and cloud-drive integrations make it especially useful for accessibility and for people who want everything read aloud.

In ForeverLM, the source already belongs to a library that includes articles, academic papers, books, podcasts, videos, courses, and meetings. You can [listen with Text to Speech](/text-to-speech), pause and [ask questions in Chat](/in-app-chat), add the source to a notebook, schedule it for today, and finish with an AI review that asks you to explain the ideas back. The audio is not a detached conversion; it stays attached to the source and its learning history.

## Where Speechify is better

- **Text-to-speech breadth.** Speechify is built around listening and accepts more ad hoc inputs, including scans and cloud documents.
- **Voice selection.** Its advertised catalog of more than 1,000 voices and 60 languages is much broader than any single ForeverLM provider.
- **Platform coverage.** Speechify works on Android, Windows, browsers, and mobile devices. ForeverLM's full library workflow is Mac-centered.
- **Very fast playback.** Premium supports speeds up to 5x; ForeverLM stops at 2x.
- **Accessibility focus.** If dyslexia, visual strain, or reading difficulty makes high-quality narration the primary need, Speechify is the more mature specialist.

## Where ForeverLM is more complete

- **One library for every learning source.** The same place holds the paper you listen to, the podcast you heard yesterday, the article you saved for Friday, and the chat where you connected their ideas.
- **Listening leads somewhere.** Schedule the source, track whether it is pending or reviewed, and use [review dialogue](/why-quizzing) to check understanding instead of treating playback as completion.
- **Chat stays integrated.** Ask about the current selection or the full source without exporting it into a separate workflow. Completed reviews remain linked to the sources they covered.
- **Choose the voice.** Pick any speech model OpenRouter serves, from many makers, and one of its voices; free ones cost nothing. You are not locked into one voice catalog or subscription.
- **Reusable audio.** Cloud narration can be cached and synced through iCloud so the iPhone can play the prepared source without regenerating every chunk.
- **Your source library stays yours.** Files and metadata live in your iCloud and local SQLite database, not in a required vendor reading account.

## Which should you choose?

Choose **Speechify** if your main question is, “What is the best way to have almost any text read aloud on all my devices?” Its specialist voice experience, language breadth, scanning, and cross-platform reach are hard to beat.

Choose **ForeverLM** if your question is, “How do I keep up with everything I want to learn, listen when reading is inconvenient, and still remember it later?” Text to Speech is included in the same system as your reading list, papers, notebooks, Schedule, source-grounded chat, and review history.

If you only need occasional narration on a Mac, start with a free voice in ForeverLM. If listening is the center of your day across Android, Windows, and the web, Speechify will probably fit better.
