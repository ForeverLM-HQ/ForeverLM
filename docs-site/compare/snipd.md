# ForeverLM vs Snipd

Snipd is the best-known AI podcast player: tap your headphones mid-episode and it captures a "snip" with transcript, AI summary, and chapter context. It's a capture tool for your ears. ForeverLM treats podcasts differently — full episodes become study sources with complete transcripts, scheduled alongside your articles and papers, and reviewed with an AI tutor afterwards. The two solve different halves of the same problem.

## At a glance

| | ForeverLM | Snipd |
|---|---|---|
| **Price** | Free to download and free over MCP; built-in AI needs a plan ($4/mo, or $19/yr for early believers) and your own OpenRouter key | Free (AI on 2 episodes/week); Premium advertised from $6.99/mo — US App Store shows ~$11.99/mo monthly, annual plans vary by region |
| **AI costs** | Your own OpenRouter key, billed by OpenRouter at cost with nothing added, plus a ForeverLM plan ($4/mo, or $19/yr for early believers) for any model in Chat; or free over MCP from an AI subscription you already pay for | Included in Premium, capped at 900 min of AI processing/month |
| **Platforms** | Native macOS app (macOS 14+) | iOS, Android, Apple Watch, CarPlay — no Mac, Windows, or web app |
| **Data storage** | Local SQLite + files in your private iCloud container | Snipd's cloud; App Store privacy label lists identity-linked usage data, including for advertising |
| **Scope** | Podcasts *plus* articles, papers, books, YouTube, X posts — one library | Podcasts only (Premium adds own audio files and YouTube) |
| **Podcast model** | Follow shows via Apple Podcasts, full episode transcripts as readable sources | Streaming player with tap-to-snip highlights, AI summaries, chapters |
| **Review method** | review dialogue with an AI tutor | None — snips export to Readwise/Notion/Obsidian for someone else to resurface |
| **Export** | Files in your iCloud Drive; SQLite you can open | Good: auto-sync of snips to Readwise, Notion, Obsidian; markdown |

*Pricing verified July 2026 — Snipd's price varies by region and billing period; check [snipd.com/pricing](https://www.snipd.com/pricing) and your local App Store.*

## Pricing: subscription with a cap vs your own AI key

Snipd Premium runs roughly **$70–$100+/yr** depending on region and billing period (the website advertises from $6.99/mo; the US App Store lists ~$11.99/mo billed monthly), with a 900-minute monthly cap on AI processing. The free tier limits AI features to two episodes a week. You pay a fixed amount and still hit a ceiling.

ForeverLM is **free to download**, and podcast transcripts are simply part of the app. What you pay for is built-in AI: a ForeverLM plan ($4 a month, or $19 a year while the early-believer offer lasts) plus your own OpenRouter key, which OpenRouter bills at its cost with nothing added. Review through the assistant you already subscribe to (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or Grok over MCP) costs nothing from ForeverLM, with your subscription covering the inference; review in the in-app Chat costs what OpenRouter bills your key for it. Transcript cleanup and other background work bills the same way.

## Where Snipd is better

- **Capture while listening.** Tap your headphones while driving or walking and the moment is saved with context. ForeverLM has no listening-time capture gesture — it's not a podcast player.
- **Mobile-first.** iPhone, Android, Apple Watch, CarPlay. ForeverLM focuses on Mac and iPhone.
- **Listening experience.** Speaker-labeled transcripts, AI chapters, episode Q&A, personalized digests — a genuinely great player.
- **Note-app integrations.** Snips flow automatically into Readwise, Notion, and Obsidian.

## Where ForeverLM is better

- **Retention actually happens.** Snipd captures highlights; it has no review system — remembering is outsourced to whatever app you export to. ForeverLM closes the loop: episodes land on your weekly Schedule and you review them in review dialogue with an AI that has the full transcript. See [Why Quizzing?](/why-quizzing)
- **The whole episode, not just the moments you caught.** A snip is the sentence you reacted to. ForeverLM ingests the complete transcript — readable, searchable, quotable in review — so the AI can quiz you on the argument, not the soundbite.
- **Podcasts beside everything else.** Your podcast episodes sit in the same library and same review loop as articles, papers, books, and videos. Snipd is podcasts-only.
- **Privacy posture.** Your library lives in your iCloud; ForeverLM servers don't store it. Snipd's processing is cloud-side, and its App Store privacy label discloses identity-linked data collection including for advertising.
- **No ceiling to hit.** Snipd stops at 900 AI minutes a month no matter what you paid, and a heavy listening month is exactly when you'd want more. ForeverLM has no cap of that kind — a busy month simply adds to your OpenRouter bill, and a quiet one adds nothing.

## Which should you choose?

Choose **Snipd** as your podcast *player* — especially if you listen on the go and want frictionless highlight capture on iPhone or Android.

Choose **ForeverLM** if the goal is *learning from* podcasts: full transcripts as study sources, scheduled review, and an AI tutor that verifies you understood the episode.

Honestly, they stack well: listen and snip in Snipd, then let ForeverLM's Apple Podcasts integration pull the same shows in as transcripts for scheduled, deeper review on your Mac.
