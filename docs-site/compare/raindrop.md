# ForeverLM vs Raindrop.io

Raindrop.io is a polished bookmark manager for saving, organizing, highlighting, searching, archiving, and sharing almost anything found online. Its generous free plan supports unlimited bookmarks, collections, highlights, and devices. ForeverLM includes a reading list, but is designed around consuming and understanding sources rather than maintaining a universal bookmark archive.

## At a glance

| | ForeverLM | Raindrop.io |
|---|---|---|
| **Price** | 7 days free, then $19 once (no subscription); AI on your own OpenRouter key at cost | Free plan; optional Pro subscription |
| **Best for** | Learning from a curated source library | Organizing a broad, durable bookmark collection |
| **Capture** | Reading lists, URLs, files, podcasts, papers, books, and plugins | Browser extensions, share sheets, apps, integrations, and uploads |
| **Organization** | Notebooks, status, source types, relationships, and Schedule | Collections, nested structure, tags, filters, highlights, and collaboration |
| **Search** | Metadata, extracted content, and semantic source search | Full-text search and permanent archive on Pro |
| **AI** | Cross-source Chat and MCP assistants | AI Assistant and suggestions on Pro |
| **Review** | Review dialogue and saved learning history | No active-review workflow |

*Raindrop.io features checked against its [official pricing page](https://raindrop.io/pro/buy) in August 2026. Local pricing is shown at checkout.*

## Where Raindrop.io is better

- **Bookmark management.** It handles a much larger universe of links, including design inspiration, shopping, tools, images, and references you may never read linearly.
- **Free cross-platform access.** Unlimited bookmarks and devices work across Mac, Windows, iOS, Android, and major browsers.
- **Collection design.** Nested collections, tags, filters, icons, sharing, and collaboration are more flexible.
- **Web preservation.** Pro keeps permanent archived copies and finds duplicate or broken links.
- **Integrations.** Thousands of automation and import/export connections make it an excellent bookmark hub.

## Where ForeverLM is better

- **Saved items become complete sources.** Articles are extracted for reading, listening, chat, and later review.
- **The library includes offline and spoken material.** Papers, books, podcasts, courses, meetings, and videos share the same notebooks.
- **The queue is intentional.** Schedule what matters rather than treating every bookmark as equal.
- **AI works across whole notebooks.** Compare arguments and retrieve evidence from the sources you are actively studying.
- **Progress has meaning.** Pending, completed, and reviewed states describe learning progress, not only an archive.

## Which should you choose?

Choose **Raindrop.io** if you want the best general-purpose bookmark organizer with generous free limits and broad platform support.

Choose **ForeverLM** if the links you save are material you intend to read, listen to, discuss, and remember.
