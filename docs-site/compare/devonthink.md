# ForeverLM vs DEVONthink

DEVONthink is a powerful document and information manager for Mac and iOS. It archives almost anything, searches large databases, links related material, automates filing, and now includes an AI chat assistant. ForeverLM is narrower: it organizes the sources you intend to learn from and connects them to listening, AI conversation, scheduling, and review.

## At a glance

| | ForeverLM | DEVONthink 4 |
|---|---|---|
| **Price** | Free; AI on your own OpenRouter key at cost | Standard $99, Pro $199, Server $499; one-time license with one year of updates |
| **Primary job** | Research library and learning loop | General document archive and information manager |
| **Storage** | Local SQLite and files in your private iCloud container | Local databases with flexible sync options |
| **AI** | Configurable in-app models plus Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Grok through MCP | AI chat with supported local or external models |
| **Automation** | Automatic source plugins and AI-operable notebooks and Schedule | Deep rules, smart groups, scripts, actions, and triggers |
| **Source types** | Learning-specific handling for papers, articles, books, podcasts, videos, courses, and meetings | Broad file, email, feed, web, scan, and document support |
| **Review** | Scheduled review dialogue with saved history | No equivalent source-understanding workflow |

*DEVONthink pricing checked against [DEVONtechnologies](https://shop.devontechnologies.com/apps/devonthink/pricing) in August 2026. Licenses do not expire, but continued updates are sold separately.*

## Where DEVONthink is better

- **Document management depth.** It is built for very large, heterogeneous archives rather than a curated learning queue.
- **Automation.** Filing rules, smart groups, scripts, OCR, triggers, and integrations can support demanding professional workflows.
- **Search and classification.** DEVONthink has decades of experience finding and relating material across databases.
- **Scanning and records.** Pro is a stronger home for receipts, email archives, legal files, and scanned office documents.
- **Flexible ownership.** Local databases, multiple sync methods, durable exports, and a perpetual license appeal to privacy-conscious Mac users.

## Where ForeverLM is better

- **It understands learning sources.** A podcast episode, paper, book, and course are not generic files. Each gets source-specific ingestion, reading, and playback behavior.
- **The queue has a purpose.** Schedule what to learn this week and track whether it was reviewed, not merely archived.
- **AI interaction is portable.** Use in-app Chat or let an external MCP assistant operate the library and save the resulting review.
- **Active understanding is built in.** Review dialogue probes gaps and preserves the session against the sources covered.
- **No license to buy.** ForeverLM is free, against $99 for DEVONthink Standard.

## Which should you choose?

Choose **DEVONthink** if you need a serious Mac document archive, advanced automation, OCR, email capture, and broad information management.

Choose **ForeverLM** if the important question is not “Where did I file this?” but “Did I understand and retain it?”

Many researchers can use both: DEVONthink as the institutional memory, ForeverLM as the active learning queue.
