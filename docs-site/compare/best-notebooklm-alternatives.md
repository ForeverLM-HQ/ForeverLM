# Best NotebookLM (Gemini Notebook) Alternatives in 2026

Google renamed NotebookLM to **Gemini Notebook** in July 2026, and it now lives at notebook.google.com. It is excellent at turning a bounded collection of sources into grounded answers, summaries, quizzes, reports, and Audio and Video Overviews. The best alternative depends on what feels limiting: isolated notebooks, source caps (50 per notebook free, 300 on Google AI Pro), Gemini-only AI, Google's cloud, usage that refills every five hours up to a weekly cap, weak citation management, or the lack of a long-term learning workflow.

There is no universal replacement. These are the strongest options by job.

## Best overall for long-term learning: ForeverLM

Both work in notebooks, but a NotebookLM notebook holds its own uploads, while a ForeverLM notebook draws on one library. Articles, papers, books, podcasts, videos, courses, and meetings accumulate in one local database, and a notebook points at the sources it uses instead of each notebook being uploaded in isolation.

The difference is clearest after the first answer. A source can be assigned to a day, read or heard, discussed with any supported model, and reviewed through a dialogue that asks you to explain it back. That review remains linked to the source. If your goal is to remember what you consume rather than generate artifacts from it, this is the strongest alternative. Moving over is one paste: a Gemini Notebook's link brings its sources, chats and notes into ForeverLM as a notebook ([Importing a Gemini Notebook](/studio#importing-a-gemini-notebook)).

## Best free study workspace: Paper

Paper does most of what Gemini Notebook does, plus FSRS flashcards, quizzes, two-host podcasts up to an hour and slide decks, inside a Notion-style editor, and it is free while in beta. Your library lives on its cloud, and its paid plans are announced but not priced.

## Best for flashcards and exams: Memo

Memo began as PDF2Anki, and cards are still its strength: FSRS scheduling, cloze and image-occlusion cards, practice tests, and export to Anki, on web, Mac, Windows, Linux and iOS. Choose it when the job is memorizing for an exam.

## Best fully local: AnythingLLM

AnythingLLM is open source and runs document chat on any of 40-odd model providers or on local models, so nothing has to leave your machine. It is a retrieval tool rather than a learning one: no flashcards, quizzes or schedule.

## Best for academic evidence: Elicit or SciSpace

Choose Elicit when you need a structured literature-review process: find papers, apply screening criteria, extract fields, and produce evidence tables. Choose SciSpace when you want a broader academic toolkit for discovery, deep reviews, PDF explanation, citations, and writing.

Neither is a direct replacement for a lifetime personal library. They are strongest before and during synthesis. ForeverLM is strongest after you have selected what matters.

## Best for citations: Papers

Papers combines a professional reference manager with PDF chat, cross-paper questions, search, related-paper discovery, collaboration, and cite-while-you-write tools. It is the better choice when the final deliverable is a manuscript or shared lab bibliography.

## Best for privacy and document control: DEVONthink

DEVONthink is the power-user choice for a private Mac archive. It handles documents, scans, mail, feeds, OCR, automation, and local databases with more depth than any AI-first research tool. Pick it when information management is the priority and you are comfortable designing your own workflow.

## How to choose

- Choose **ForeverLM** to keep, discuss, listen to, schedule, and remember sources across formats.
- Choose **Paper** for a free study workspace with flashcards and podcasts.
- Choose **Memo** for exam flashcards that export to Anki.
- Choose **AnythingLLM** for document chat on local models.
- Choose **Elicit** for systematic reviews and structured evidence extraction.
- Choose **SciSpace** for academic discovery and PDF explanation.
- Choose **Papers** for citations, shared scholarly libraries, and AI across papers.
- Choose **DEVONthink** for a private, automated Mac document archive.
- Stay with **Gemini Notebook** if Gemini, generated artifacts, and easy cloud notebooks already solve the job.
