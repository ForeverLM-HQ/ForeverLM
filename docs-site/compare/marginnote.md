# ForeverLM vs MarginNote

MarginNote 4 combines PDF and EPUB reading, highlighting, mind maps, flashcards, OCR, video notes, and AI assistance on Apple devices. It is one of the closest comparisons for students who want reading and review in the same product. ForeverLM trades MarginNote's detailed visual annotation system for broader source capture, cross-source AI, podcasts, listening, and an assistant-accessible Schedule.

## At a glance

| | ForeverLM | MarginNote 4 |
|---|---|---|
| **Price** | Free, MCP included; AI on your own OpenRouter key at cost | Pro $12.99 lifetime on iPhone/iPad; Max $16.99/year or $51.99 lifetime |
| **Platforms** | Mac-centered with synced iPhone access | Mac, iPhone, and iPad, depending on plan |
| **Reading** | Papers, articles, books, and transcripts | Deep PDF and EPUB reading with OCR and visual extraction |
| **Knowledge structure** | Notebooks, source relationships, search, and Schedule | One card can be a highlight, mind-map node, and flashcard |
| **AI** | Configurable models, source chat, and external MCP assistants | Credit-based summary, Q&A, OCR, translation, and card generation |
| **Review** | Review dialogue grounded in complete sources | Flashcards, FSRS, document recall, and mind-map recall |
| **Beyond documents** | Podcasts, videos, courses, meetings, and X posts | Local and online video notes, but primarily document-centered |

*MarginNote pricing and AI credit rules checked against its [official pricing page](https://www.marginnote.com/en/pricing/) in August 2026.*

## Where MarginNote is better

- **Annotation becomes structure.** A single excerpt can appear as a document highlight, mind-map node, and flashcard.
- **Visual study.** Mind maps, outlines, links, and extended notes make relationships explicit.
- **Handwriting and OCR.** It is designed for iPad study with scanned material and Apple Pencil.
- **Several recall modes.** FSRS cards, hidden annotations, and progressive mind-map reveal support different kinds of rehearsal.
- **Low one-time pricing.** Lifetime options are unusually affordable, though AI remains credit-metered.

## Where ForeverLM is better

- **More source streams arrive automatically.** Articles, papers, podcasts, books, videos, courses, meetings, and plugins share one library.
- **Podcasts are first-class.** Follow shows, keep episode transcripts, and review them like any other source.
- **AI can operate across the system.** MCP assistants can retrieve sources, plan days, move work, and save review transcripts.
- **Conversation tests explanation.** Review emphasizes articulating the full idea rather than recognizing a card answer.
- **Data and model flexibility.** Source files stay in your iCloud, and the model can be chosen per task.

## Which should you choose?

Choose **MarginNote** if you study primarily from PDFs and EPUBs and want a tightly integrated system of annotation, mind maps, and flashcards.

Choose **ForeverLM** if your learning spans many formats and you want the library, AI assistants, schedule, listening, and conversational review to work as one system.
