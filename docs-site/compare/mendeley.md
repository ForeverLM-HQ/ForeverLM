# ForeverLM vs Mendeley

Mendeley is Elsevier's reference manager for storing, organizing, annotating, sharing, and citing academic papers. Its paid plans now add a Reading Assistant, cross-library questions, experiment comparison, and larger storage. ForeverLM also manages and chats with papers, but treats them as one part of a broader research and learning library.

## At a glance

| | ForeverLM | Mendeley |
|---|---|---|
| **Price** | 7 days free, then $19 once (no subscription); AI on your own OpenRouter key at cost | Free with 2 GB; Plus $55/year; Pro $110/year; Max $165/year |
| **Paper storage** | Local files and metadata in private iCloud | Elsevier cloud, from 2 GB to unlimited by plan |
| **AI** | Configurable models, multi-source Chat, and MCP assistants | Reading Assistant; Ask My Library and Compare Experiments on Pro and Max |
| **Citations** | No cite-while-you-write plugin | Mendeley Cite for Microsoft Word |
| **Organization** | Notebooks, tags, source status, Schedule, and relationships | Collections, tags, annotations, notebook, and research groups |
| **Beyond papers** | Articles, books, podcasts, videos, courses, and meetings | Academic references and research papers |
| **Review** | Source-linked review dialogue | No comparable learning schedule and review history |

*Mendeley pricing and AI features checked against its [official pricing page](https://www.mendeley.com/pricing/) in August 2026.*

## Where Mendeley is better

- **Citation workflow.** Mendeley Cite inserts references and bibliographies in Word.
- **Research discovery.** Recommendations and Elsevier's scholarly ecosystem help find related work.
- **Generous free entry.** The free reference manager includes 2 GB of storage and limited Reading Assistant questions.
- **Cross-platform academic workflow.** Desktop, web, and browser tools support researchers outside the Apple ecosystem.
- **Collaboration.** Research groups and shared material fit labs and course teams.

## Where ForeverLM is more complete

- **Every research format belongs together.** A paper can share a notebook with interviews, lectures, articles, books, and meeting notes.
- **The library is not tied to a publisher cloud.** Files and metadata remain local and in your private iCloud container.
- **AI choice is explicit.** Select models per task or use an assistant subscription you already have through MCP.
- **Papers can be listened to.** Narration, source chat, and reading stay attached to the same record.
- **Understanding is tracked.** Schedule the paper, run a review, and keep the transcript and completion state.

## Which should you choose?

Choose **Mendeley** if you want a cloud reference manager with Word citations, scholarly recommendations, collaboration, and Elsevier-connected research services.

Choose **ForeverLM** if citation formatting is secondary to reading, listening, connecting, discussing, and retaining the paper alongside everything else you learn.
