# Kindle books

ForeverLM cannot import a Kindle book. Amazon protects what it sells with DRM, and ForeverLM does not remove it. A DRM-free file is the only kind of Kindle book it can add, and Amazon sells few of those.

Some users have reported success taking a screenshot of each page of a Kindle book, then asking their AI agent to convert the pages into an EPUB. ForeverLM does not support or recommend this. Amazon's terms of service do not allow extracting a book's pages from the Kindle app, and taking those screenshots is itself against them, so you do this at your own risk.

If you do, keep the EPUB to yourself. Never share or distribute it: that is not allowed, and ForeverLM will not help you do it.

For books you can add, see [Books](/books#adding-a-book).
